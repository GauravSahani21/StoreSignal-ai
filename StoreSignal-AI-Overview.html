Project Overview

What is StoreSignal AI?

StoreSignal AI is a physical-retail intelligence platform built for automotive dealerships. It captures showroom conversations via audio ingestion, segments them into structured dialogue turns, and runs a multi-agent AI pipeline to coach floor advisors, resolve shopper identities, and draft human-reviewed follow-up outreach — with strict privacy governance, zero hallucination guarantees, and full audit traceability.

Core Features

Six Intelligent Modules

Each module maps to a dedicated page in the web application and addresses a specific stage of the in-store retail intelligence pipeline.

📊
Module 1

Retail Intelligence & Analytics

Real-time showroom metrics — conversation counts, shopper identity, conversion rates, attributed revenue, AI latency, and operational cost telemetry.

  • Filterable dashboard with multi-store location support
  • Conversation Intelligence Workspace with dual-pane inspector
  • Intent score, workflow state, and shopper linkage per conversation
  • Export Events CSV for offline reporting
🎙️
Module 2

Showroom Audio Studio

Live hardware acoustic feed ingestion with speaker diarization, verbal consent attestation, and multi-turn dialogue segmentation engine.

  • Simulated recording with staggered waveform visualizer
  • Paste or load dealership preset transcripts (3 vehicles)
  • Normalizes timestamps and speaker tags into addressable segments
  • Consent verified and recorded before any data is stored
🤖
Module 3

Floor Coach & Multi-Agent Workflow

Real-time advisor coaching powered by a governed four-agent orchestration DAG — Retrieval → (FloorCoach ∥ SignalAnalyst) → FollowUpOptimizer.

  • FloorCoach: live talking points with cited knowledge base facts
  • SignalAnalyst: objection detection and purchase intent scoring
  • FollowUpOptimizer: channel selection and outreach draft generation
  • Human-in-the-loop approval queue; no automated dispatch
👤
Module 4

Shoppers & Cross-Visit Continuity

Privacy-first shopper identity resolution, cross-visit memory, dealership outcome logger, and full DPDP Right-to-Erasure compliance tools.

  • Resolve by explicit customer ID or isolated anonymous session
  • Track returning visitors with purchase and objection history
  • Record outcomes: purchase, missed conversion, no-response
  • One-click consent revocation and permanent profile erasure
🧪
Module 5

Campaign Experimentation Engine

Statistical A/B testing platform for sales scripts, vehicle feature emphasis, and follow-up channel strategies across showroom advisors.

  • Live experiment cards with N-count and conversion rate stats
  • Statistical governance gates (minimum sample size before winner)
  • Import Outcomes CSV for batch result ingestion
  • Causality disclaimers for physical in-store factor awareness
⚙️
Module 6

System Status & AI Gateway

Enterprise LLM connection diagnostics, live latency telemetry, infrastructure health dashboard, and interactive Groq test console.

  • Real-time Groq LPU connectivity and latency measurement
  • Fastify server, vector index, and governance engine status
  • Execute test prompts directly against the live Groq API
  • Security: TLS 1.3, PII redact guard, zero training data retention

Governance & Compliance

Privacy-First by Design

StoreSignal AI enforces strict data governance at every layer — from consent attestation at capture time to mandatory human review before any outreach, aligned with India's DPDP Act and ISO 42001.

🛡️
Verbal Consent Attestation
Customer consent is verified and recorded before any audio is captured or processed. Consent method, scope, and timestamp stored in an immutable audit ledger.
👁️
Human-in-the-Loop Review
All AI-generated outreach drafts require advisor approval before any customer contact. Automated blast dispatch is architecturally prohibited — not just policy.
🔒
Zero Hallucination Guarantee
Every vehicle specification cited by an AI agent is retrieved from a verified RAG chunk with strict source citation tags. Unverified claims are blocked at the agent layer.
🗑️
Right to Erasure (DPDP)
One-click profile erasure permanently deletes all personal data and unlinks derived records — compliant with India's DPDP Act Section 12.
🔐
TLS 1.3 + PII Redact
All data encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3. A PII sanitisation guard runs on all AI outputs to strip personal identifiers before storage or display.
📋
Multi-Tenant RBAC Isolation
Organisation-level partitioning (orgId) with strict cross-tenant access denial, verified by automated RBAC tests in the test suite.
📊
Statistical Governance for Experiments
The Campaign Experimentation Engine enforces minimum sample-size gates before declaring A/B winners, preventing premature decisions. Guardrail warnings and causality disclaimers note that physical in-store factors (foot traffic, inventory levels) can influence conversion rates beyond AI-driven signals.
