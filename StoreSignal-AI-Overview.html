Executive Summary

Bridging the Physical Retail Intelligence Gap

Physical car dealerships lose over 68% of showroom walk-ins due to unassisted sales objections, delayed follow-up, and lost context across visits. StoreSignal AI converts spoken showroom dialogues into structured commercial intelligence in real time.

The Physical Dealership Problem
Traditional Dealership Deficiencies
  • Uncaptured Objections: Real customer hesitation (battery anxiety, charging wait, EMI financing) is lost once the customer exits the door.
  • Inconsistent Floor Knowledge: Advisors struggle with intricate EV specifications, warranty nuances, and competitor comparisons on the spot.
  • Generic, Delayed Follow-Up: CRM blast messages are sent days later with no memory of what the shopper specifically debated.
StoreSignal AI Autonomous Solution
Autonomous Multi-Agent Value Engine
  • Real-Time Floor Coaching: Instant battle cards and verified facts prompt advisors while the customer is standing right next to the car.
  • Zero-Hallucination Citations: Answers are retrieved strictly from verified 24-chunk dealership technical manuals.
  • Tailored Follow-Up Drafts: Pre-crafted WhatsApp & Email outreach ready for 1-click advisor approval within 60 seconds of exit.

Section 04 · Agentic DAG & Reasoning Pipeline
Agentic Workflow Engineering

Governed 4-Agent Execution DAG

Unlike chaotic chat agents, StoreSignal AI decomposes retail intelligence into a deterministic Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with explicit responsibilities, citation schemas, and security guardrails.

Agent 1: RAG Retrieval Specialist
Stage: Knowledge Grounding (Pre-Inference)
Extracts conversational entities (e.g. "Vayu X1 battery", "home charger installation cost", "Nexon warranty comparison") and performs cosine similarity retrieval over 24 verified dealership knowledge chunks.
Output: { chunks: [chunk_04_battery, chunk_11_warranty], score: 0.89 }
Agent 2: Floor Coach Agent
Stage: Real-Time Floor Advisor Guidance
Analyzes the shopper's most recent statement in combination with retrieved RAG chunks to formulate concise, 1-sentence verbal prompts for the advisor. Strict prohibition against citing unverified claims.
Output: { cue: "Highlight 8-year battery warranty vs Nexon's 5-year", citation: "CHUNK_11" }
Agent 3: Signal Analyst Agent
Stage: Psychological & Intent Classification
Parses vocal tone cues, objection frequency, price sensitivity signals, and timeline urgency. Quantifies customer intent into a normalized 0–100 integer score and tags key friction points.
Output: { intentScore: 78, friction: "Range Anxiety", sentiment: "Hesitant" }
Agent 4: Follow-Up Optimizer Agent
Stage: Governed Outreach Draft Synthesis
Synthesizes dialogue history, resolved objections, and intent scores into personalized follow-up messages across WhatsApp, Email, or SMS with customizable dealership incentive offers.
Output: { channel: "WhatsApp", draft: "Hi Rahul, following up on your Vayu X1 range test...", status: "PENDING_APPROVAL" }
Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance Safeguard
Zero Autonomous Message Dispatch Policy
Architectural Enforcement

To preserve customer trust and prevent brand degradation, StoreSignal AI enforces a strict architectural boundary: no AI agent has network authorization to transmit communications directly to a customer. Every generated outreach draft is deposited into the Human Approval Queue. The showroom advisor must review, edit if necessary, and explicitly press "Approve & Send".
